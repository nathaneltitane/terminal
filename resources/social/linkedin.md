# Terminal

Terminal is an evolving collection of shell scripts, templates, system
configurations, dotfiles and utilities designed to simplify and enhance
the Linux experience. Built around customization, automation and
open-source tools, it brings together practical solutions for system
management, desktop environments, development workflows and mobile Linux
environments through Termux.

More than just a collection of scripts, Terminal is a continuously
developed toolkit shaped by real-world needs, with an emphasis on
flexibility, consistency and making complex setups easier to manage and
reproduce.
